How this is calculated, the basket, and the wage orders used

The measure

Hours needed = weekly basket cost ÷ hourly minimum wage, where the hourly wage is the daily non-agriculture minimum wage ÷ 8. For each month, the wage used is the latest wage order in force on the 15th, since PSA prices are averages over the month.

The basket

An illustrative week of basic food for a family of five, built from items PSA prices in every region. It is not an official PSA food threshold.

What is left out

  • Minimum wage is not actual take-home pay: many workers earn more, and informal workers often earn less.
  • The basket covers fresh food only. Cooking oil, sugar, milk, bread and canned goods are not in PSA's agricultural price files.
  • When PSA has no price for an item in a region (for example eggs and red onion in Central Visayas), the average of the other regions that month is used and marked with *.
  • Agriculture and small-business wage rates are lower than the non-agriculture rate used here, so hours needed are higher for those workers.

Data: PSA OpenStat retail prices of agricultural commodities; DOLE NWPC regional wage orders. Cleaned with Python (pandas), analyzed in SQLite. Code and SQL are in this repository.
